Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

AEGIS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, and used when you use the AEGIS application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google account information, such as your email address and profile details.
  • Prescription images uploaded or captured through the application.
  • Information extracted from prescriptions, including patient name, date, case sheet reference, medicine name, and dose or quantity.
  • Information required to maintain authentication sessions.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate users through Google Sign-In.
  • Read and extract information from prescription images.
  • Organize extracted information into spreadsheet rows.
  • Save or export prescription data through Google Sheets.
  • Maintain application functionality and security.

3. Third-Party Services

AEGIS uses third-party services to provide its functionality. These may include:

  • Google OAuth for authentication.
  • Google Sheets for spreadsheet storage and management.
  • An AI processing provider for prescription image extraction.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers for application operation.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

4. Data Storage and Security

Information is stored or processed using the services configured for the application. We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party providers described in this policy to operate the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as needed to provide the application. Users may contact us to request access to or deletion of their information. Some information may remain in third-party services or backups until it is deleted through those services.

7. Children's Privacy

AEGIS is not intended for independent use by children. Users must have appropriate authorization to upload or process another person's prescription information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
